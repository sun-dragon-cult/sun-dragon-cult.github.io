RuneQuest measures everything in meters: a movement unit (MOV) is 3 meters, and spell ranges and
areas are given in meters. The system sets new scenes to 1.5 meters per grid space. Keep the scene's
grid units in meters, as the movement ruler and spell areas count in them.

## Hexes, squares or gridless

All three work:

- **Hexes** are the common choice for RuneQuest maps. Distance is counted hex by hex.
- **Squares** need one Foundry setting to match the rules, see below.
- **Gridless** scenes measure straight lines in meters. Spell areas then show as plain circles
  instead of lit-up spaces, and nothing snaps to a grid.

## Diagonals on square grids

Foundry's default, **Equidistant**, counts a diagonal step as one space, the same as a straight one.
A human with MOV 8 (24 meters) could then move 16 squares diagonally, about 34 meters. Spell areas
always measure true distance, so a 3 meter Harmony wouldn't reach the squares the ruler calls 3
meters away diagonally.

Set **Configure Settings → Core → Grid Diagonals** to **Exact**. A diagonal step then counts as
about 2.1 meters, which matches the rules, and the movement ruler and spell areas agree. It's a
world setting, so it covers every scene. The system doesn't change it for you, since that would
change movement in worlds already in play.

## The movement ruler

When a token in combat is dragged, its ruler is colored by how far it moves, from its MOV:

- **Green** up to half its movement, when it can still attack.
- **Yellow** up to its full movement.
- **Orange** beyond its full movement.
- **Red** beyond its sprint, when the optional sprint rule is on: set **Sprint Range Multiplier** in
  the system's token ruler settings.
