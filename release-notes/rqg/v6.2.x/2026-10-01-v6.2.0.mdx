This release's headline feature is spell effects. Cast a spell such as Bladesharp, Protection or
Strength, and when it takes effect, the target's owner clicks Apply on the chat card to put the
spell's effect on the right actor or weapon, with the right level and duration. Casting the same
spell again, or a spell that can't share a target with one already there, is handled for you, and
every spell on a token shows at the top of the token HUD, where the GM can open, remove or add one.

It builds on resisted spells: a spell like Sleep or Befuddle now hands the target's owner a card to
resist or accept, and rolls POW against POW on the resistance table.

Active Effects get more capable too: an effect on an actor can now change the actor's items, such as
one skill, every hit location, or the weapon it's on.

Alongside that, a new actor can start from a template actor, and many more things can be dragged to
the hotbar.

### Casting and applying

<GithubIssue issue="1078" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

The spell card now shows one row per target, saying whether the spell took effect. If the outcome
needs a judgment call, the GM can rule on each row: takes effect, or no effect. When the spell took
effect, the target's owner gets an **Apply** button. Clicking it puts the spell's effect on the
target:

- A spell that works on a weapon, like Bladesharp or Fireblade, goes on the target's equipped weapon
  it fits, and asks which one if there's more than one.
- Any other spell goes on the target itself.
- The effect is named after the spell and its level, like "Bladesharp (3)", and lasts the spell's
  duration: 2 minutes for spirit magic, 15 minutes for rune magic. A few effects have a longer
  duration of their own, like Catseye's 12 hours.

### Which spells have effects

The spell effects ship with wiki module 2.1.0, which has an effect for every spirit and rune spell
that lasts for a while. Some change the sheet, like Strength raising STR or Bladesharp adding to the
weapon's attack and damage. The others are reminders that show the spell is active until it runs
out. See the [Wiki 2.1.0 release notes](https://sun-dragon-cult.github.io/release-notes/wiki-v2.1.0)
for the full list.

:::warning Update the wiki module first

Update the RQG wiki module to 2.1.0 **before** the system. When the world is upgraded, the migration
links the spells already on your actors to their effects by looking them up in the wiki module's
compendiums. If the wiki module is still the old version, those spells get no effect link. If that
happens, update the wiki module and run the migration again: as the GM, click Trigger World
Migration in the Game Settings sidebar tab. The world reloads and migrates again, and this time the
spells get their links. Links that are already there are left alone, so running it again is safe.

:::

### Recasting and incompatible spells

By the rules, casting a spell on a target that already has the same spell only works if the new
casting is stronger, and some spells can't be on the same target at once. For example, Bladesharp
and Fireblade can't share a weapon, and neither can Protection and Shimmer on a person. When a spell
has no effect for one of these reasons, Apply tells you why and the card records it. Only the
target's owner sees the reason.

The world setting **Recast Spells Take Effect** chooses the rule: **Only if stronger** (the rules as
written) or **Always**, where every new casting replaces the old one.

### Spell effects on the token

A token's spell effects are shown at the top of the token HUD's status palette, above the condition
markers, with their name, the item they're on and the time left. The GM can click one to open it,
right-click to remove it, or change its level on the effect's sheet.

The GM can also add a spell effect without casting, for example for a spell cast before the scene
started. Click the **+** in the same section, search for the spell, set its level, and click Add.

{/* TODO screenshot: the token HUD with the spell effect section and the + picker */}

### Token status markers

Because spells now show on tokens as spell effects, the token HUD's status palette no longer holds
spell markers. Protection, Strength, Bladesharp and the other spell statuses are gone, and so are
Ironhand, Parry and the 1–6 counters. The counters were a way to note a spell's level, which the
spell effect's name now shows.

The palette now has general condition markers only. The new ones are Helpless, Concentrating, In
Spirit Combat, Discorporate, Possessed, Invisible, Flying and Burning, and Restrained is now called
Immobilized.

:::note Upgrading

A spell status that is already on a token when you upgrade stays on it as an ordinary effect until
it runs out. You can also delete it from the actor's Effects tab. For a spell that has no spell
effect yet, add a plain effect on the actor's Effects tab.

:::

## Active Effects That Change an Actor's Items

<GithubIssue issue="920" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

Active Effects that change a value on the actor's items have a new, more versatile key format.
Before, such a change had to use the **Custom** change type and could only add to the value. Now it
can use all of Foundry's change types. For example, it can raise one skill, add armor to every hit
location, or improve the weapon it's on. Start the change's key with `@`, followed by which items to
change, a `:`, and the path of the value:

| Key                       | Changes                                              | Example                                                 |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `@<rqid>:system.<path>`   | The actor's item with that rqid                      | `@i.skill.dodge:system.baseChance`, add 10              |
| `@~<regex>:system.<path>` | Every item whose rqid matches the regular expression | `@~^i\.hit-location\.:system.naturalAp`, add 2          |
| `@.:system.<path>`        | The item the effect is on                            | `@.:system.effect.add.melee.attack` on a weapon, add 10 |

- **Change types:** these changes use Foundry's normal types, Add, Multiply, Override, Upgrade and
  Downgrade, just like any other key. Keys that don't start with `@` work exactly as they do in
  Foundry.
- **Weapon bonuses:** the `system.effect.add` values are bonuses added on top of a value. A weapon
  has them for melee and missile attack, parry and damage. The damage bonus is new in this release,
  and a weapon's rolled damage never goes below 0.
- **Spell level:** in an effect that comes from a spell, `@level` in the value is the level the
  spell was cast at. For example, Bladesharp adds `@level * 5` to attack.
- **Warnings:** a key that can't be used, such as a mistyped rqid, an rqid the actor has no item
  for, or a change type a bonus can't use, shows the GM a warning saying what's wrong. When the
  world loads, these are collected into one summary.

See [rqid: Active Effects](/rqg-system/api/rqid#7-active-effects) for the bonus values you can
change and more examples.

:::warning Changed keys

Before this release, a key like `i.skill.dodge:system.baseChance` on the **Custom** change type
changed an item. The world migration rewrites these to the new form,
`@i.skill.dodge:system.baseChance` on **Add**, so they keep doing the same thing. A key in the old
form that the migration can't reach, such as one typed afterwards or in a module's compendium, shows
the GM a warning and does nothing until it's updated.

:::

## Resisted Spells and Resistance Rolls

<GithubIssue issue="757" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
<GithubIssue issue="758" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
<GithubIssue issue="1066" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

A spell that the target can resist, like Sleep, Befuddle or Slow, now checks its target before any
magic points are spent. It asks for confirmation if you have no target (a cast on yourself), and
stops if you have more than one. The cast then posts a single card to the target's owner, who
chooses **Resist** or **Accept**. The resistance roll pits the caster's POW against the target's POW
on the resistance table. The target doesn't see which spell it is until it takes effect.

The GM can also send any resistance roll to a player, for example STR against a trap, from the actor
sheet's Combat tab, the combat tracker, the Actors sidebar, or a Token HUD button. The Token HUD
button can be turned off in the settings.

## Start a New Actor from a Template

<GithubIssue issue="636" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
<GithubIssue issue="778" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
<GithubIssue issue="1055" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

Foundry's **Create Actor** dialog has a new **Start from** choice. **Blank** works as before, and
**Choose template...** opens a list of template actors from the world and the compendiums. The wiki
module provides templates for adventurers, like "Human (Base)" and "Mostali (Dwarf)", and for
creatures, like animals, chaos monsters, elementals, mounts and spirits. Search the list by name, or
filter it by class, role and species. The new actor is a copy of the template, with all its items.

See [Creating an Adventurer](/rqg-system/getting-started/creating-actor) for how to make your own
actors show up as templates.

## Hotbar Drag-and-Drop

<GithubIssue issue="402" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
<GithubIssue issue="874" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
<GithubIssue issue="1059" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
<GithubIssue issue="1060" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

- A **weapon** on the hotbar now opens its attack dialog, and a **spirit or rune magic spell** opens
  its cast dialog.
- Items on the actor sheet's Combat tab, including Dodge, can be dragged to the hotbar.
- A **compendium** on the hotbar opens it, a **journal page** opens its journal at that page, and a
  **scene** views it.
- Ammunition that can't attack on its own, like arrows, and folders show a warning instead of making
  a macro that doesn't work.

## API for Macros and Modules

<GithubIssue issue="1129" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
<GithubIssue issue="1131" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

The system now has a documented API, `game.system.api`, for macros and modules. A macro can roll
skills, runes, passions and characteristics, attack with a weapon, and cast spirit and rune magic,
with the roll dialog or without it. It can also read a character's skills, weapon attacks and the
spells they can cast, including spells from an Allied Spirit, a bound spirit or a spell matrix.
Values stored on a character, like hit points and magic points, are read straight from the character
data, which is now also part of the API.

See the [API overview](/rqg-system/api) to get started, and the
[macro examples](/rqg-system/api/macro-examples) for ready-to-use macros. From now on, changes that
can break macros or modules are listed in the release notes.

## Old Sheets Will Be Removed in 6.3.0

The newer character and item sheets are the default. Their names end in **V2**, like "Character
Sheet V2". The old sheets, like "Character Sheet" and "Passion Item Sheet", can still be picked in a
document's **Sheet** configuration, but they will be removed in RQG 6.3.0. Actors and items set to
an old sheet then open in the V2 sheet automatically, so there's nothing you need to do.

## For Content Creators

- Spells link to their effect with an **Effect** field on the spell sheet, next to the description
  link. Spells already on actors get their link from the compendium during the world migration.
  <GithubIssue issue="1086" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
- Spells have a **Resisted By** field that says how a target can resist them. So far, only a
  resistance roll of POW against POW is supported; the other options are shown but not used yet.
  <GithubIssue issue="1068" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
- Spells record what they can be cast on and what kind of effect they have, in two new fields.
  <GithubIssue issue="1080" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
- Errors and warnings now carry a code like `RQG-W-0012`, which makes them easier to report and look
  up.
