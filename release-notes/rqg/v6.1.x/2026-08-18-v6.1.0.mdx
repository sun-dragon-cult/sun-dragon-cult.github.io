This release's headline feature is a real magic point source picker: casting a spell now lets you
choose where the points come from whenever more than one source is available. POW-storing crystals
work as an alternate source instead of the old max-boosting hack, a Rune Priest's Allied Spirit can
share magic points, Rune points, and spell knowledge with its priest, spirits trapped in an item via
a Binding Enchantment can be drawn on the same way, and a Spell Matrix Enchantment lets a Spirit
Magic spell travel with a physical item for anyone holding it to cast.

Alongside that, resolving a season's worth of pending Experience checks no longer means chasing
across the Skills, Rune, and Passion tabs to find every one and opening a separate dialog for each.
A new Experience Roll Session lists everything pending at once with an optional Roll All action.

## Magic Point Source Picker

<GithubIssue issue="956" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

Any Gear, Weapon, or Armor item can now carry stored magic points (Core p.263's Magic Point
Enchantment) - the shape a POW-storing crystal takes in this system, though nothing stops it being
used for any other item that fits. Once a GM marks an item's stored points identified, casting a
Spirit or Rune Magic spell lets the caster choose where the points are drawn from: their own pool, a
storage item, an Allied Spirit bond, or "Auto," which drains through all of them in a single
GM/player-defined order - the caster's own pool is just one entry in that order, not a special
fallback after it. That order - and which items show up as sources at all - is managed from a new
Magic Point Sources popout on the character sheet, with drag-and-drop reordering.

This replaces the old approach of bumping an actor's Magic Point max via an Active Effect on the
item, which merged a crystal's points into the owner's own pool instead of keeping them as a
separate, spendable reserve. Any item still using the old approach is migrated automatically the
next time your world runs its migrations - no manual cleanup needed.

:::caution Manual cleanup needed if you tracked crystal charges via Quantity

If you instead house-ruled a crystal's remaining points by hand using its Quantity field (e.g.
Quantity 5 meaning 5 points left, decrementing it as points were spent), that pattern isn't covered
by the automatic migration - there's nothing in the data for it to recognize. Set the item's new
Stored Magic Points value/max yourself, then set Quantity back to 1 - and consider switching the
item's type to Unique if you no longer need it to behave as a Consumable.

:::

## Allied Spirits

<GithubIssue issue="957" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />
<GithubIssue issue="1002" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

A Rune Priest's Allied Spirit (Core p.277) is now more than flavor text. Link the bond partner from
the priest's Combat tab, and from then on - in either direction - the pair share Magic Points, Rune
Points for any cult they're both initiated to, and known Spirit Magic and Rune Magic spells. That
means a priest can cast a spell their Allied Spirit knows (or vice versa) by drawing on the other's
points, even for spells they never personally learned - shown on the caster's own Spirit/Rune Magic
tab under a "From \{name\}:" divider so it's clear the knowledge isn't really theirs.

The bond is capped at one per priest, matching RAW, but that cap is GM-adjudicated rather than
blocked in the UI - same as everywhere else this release leaves a soft RAW limit to the table rather
than hard-coding it.

:::info

Rune Point sharing matches the priest's and Allied Spirit's cults by their Rqid - something cults
never needed before this release, so an older cult item in your world may not have one. The Rune
Magic casting dialog now tells you explicitly when a bonded Allied Spirit exists but isn't
recognized as sharing a cult, instead of the source picker just silently not appearing, and the Rqid
batch editor now covers cults too, so it can fix this for you.

<GithubIssue issue="1004" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

:::

## Bound Spirits

<GithubIssue issue="999" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

A spirit trapped in an item via a Binding Enchantment (Core p.249) can now be linked from that
item's sheet by dragging the spirit's actor onto it. Once bound, whoever holds the item can draw on
the spirit's Magic Points during casting - alongside storage crystals and an Allied Spirit bond in
the same source picker - and can cast any Spirit Magic spell the spirit itself knows, shown under
its own "From \{spirit\} in \{item\}:" divider.

Draining a bound spirit's Magic Points to zero releases it from the binding rather than destroying
it (per the Well of Daliath's correction to Weapons & Equipment p.120), and a cast that would do
that prompts for confirmation first. The RAW cap of CHA÷3 total bound spirits (RQG p.250) is shown
as an informational count on the Combat tab, GM-adjudicated rather than enforced.

## Spell Matrix Enchantments

<GithubIssue issue="959" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

Any Gear, Weapon, or Armor item can now carry a Spell Matrix Enchantment (Core p.264-265): drag a
Spirit Magic spell onto the item's sheet to enchant it. The item only stores a link to that spell (a
Rqid) plus the number of points enchanted into it - the spell itself is never embedded on the item
or copied onto it. Every time the matrix is cast, the system looks up that Rqid fresh against
whatever world or compendium item currently matches it, so a later correction to the spell (or an
update to the compendium it came from) is picked up automatically instead of the matrix carrying a
stale copy - the same reason Rqid already backs the rest of the system's cross-references.

Whoever is holding the item can cast the stored spell straight from the actor sheet - a gear-row
indicator for a quick roll, or its own entry on the Spirit Magic tab under a "Matrix:" divider,
alongside the existing "Allied Spirit:" and "Bound Spirit:" dividers, which are now labeled
distinctly instead of sharing generic "From ..." wording.

Casting a matrix spell uses the caster's own POW×5 chance and their own Magic Points (through the
same source picker as any other spell), matching Core's rule that access to the spell - not its
casting cost - is what the item grants. It doesn't count against the holder's own CHA spell-point
limit, since it was never one of their personally-learned spells. If the same item also carries a
Magic Point Enchantment (#956), its stored points already show up as an ordinary source in the
picker for any spell, matrix-granted or not - no separate wiring needed to combine the two.

## Experience Roll Session

<GithubIssue issue="912" repo="fvtt-system-rqg" />

A new button on the actor sheet header opens a single-screen session listing every pending
Experience check on the character at once - skills, Runes, Passions, and POW - with an optional Roll
All action and a fixed/random gain toggle, instead of working through each one's own Improve dialog
in turn.

Rolling through the per-item Improve dialogs (for Training and Research, which stay there) and the
new session both now go through the same shared resolver, so a gain roll behaves identically either
way - including a fix along the way for negative gain rolls incorrectly shrinking a value, and for
Rune/Passion improvements not respecting their 100% cap.
