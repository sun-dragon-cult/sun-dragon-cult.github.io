RQG 6.2.0

October 1, 2026 · 11 min read

This release's headline feature is spell effects. Cast a spell such as Bladesharp, Protection or Strength, and when it takes effect, the target's owner clicks Apply on the chat card to put the spell's effect on the right actor or weapon, with the right level and duration. Casting the same spell again, or a spell that can't share a target with one already there, is handled for you, and every spell on a token shows at the top of the token HUD, where the GM can open, remove or add one.

It builds on resisted spells: a spell like Sleep or Befuddle now hands the target's owner a card to resist or accept, and rolls POW against POW on the resistance table.

Active Effects get more capable too: an effect on an actor can now change the actor's items, such as one skill, every hit location, or the weapon it's on.

Alongside that, a new actor can start from a template actor, and many more things can be dragged to the hotbar.

Spell Effects​

Casting and applying​

The spell card now shows one row per target, saying whether the spell took effect. If the outcome needs a judgment call, the GM can rule on each row: takes effect, or no effect. When the spell took effect, the target's owner gets an Apply button. Clicking it puts the spell's effect on the target:

  • A spell that works on a weapon (Bladesharp, Bludgeon, Dullblade, Fireblade) goes on the target's equipped weapon it fits, and asks which one if there's more than one.
  • Any other spell goes on the target itself.
  • The effect is named after the spell and its level, like "Bladesharp (3)", and lasts the spell's duration: 2 minutes for spirit magic, 15 minutes for rune magic.

Which spells have effects​

The spell effects ship with the wiki module.

Update the wiki module first

Update the RQG wiki module before the system. When the world is upgraded, the migration links the spells already on your actors to their effects by looking them up in the wiki module's compendiums. If the wiki module is still the old version, those spells get no effect link. If that happens, update the wiki module and run the migration again: as the GM, click Trigger World Migration in the Game Settings sidebar tab. The world reloads and migrates again, and this time the spells get their links. Links that are already there are left alone, so running it again is safe.

  • With game effects:
    • Strength, Vigor, Coordination, Glamour: raise STR, CON, DEX, CHA. Damage bonus, hit points and skill modifiers follow.
    • Protection: armor on every hit location.
    • Bladesharp, Bludgeon, Dullblade: attack and damage on the weapon.
    • Fireblade: the weapon's damage becomes 3D6 plus damage bonus.
    • Mobility, Slow: double or halve MOV.
    • Light, Lantern: the token gives off light.
  • Reminders: some spells change nothing that the sheet can calculate, and show that the spell is active until it runs out. These are Befuddle, Countermagic, Demoralize, Fanaticism, Farsee, Forget, Glue, Shimmer, Sleep and Spirit Screen, and the rune spells Become Other Sex, Clairvoyance, Discorporation, Impede Chaos, Mind Read, Path Watch, Soul Sight, Truespeak and Wind Words.

More spells will get effects in later releases.

Recasting and incompatible spells​

By the rules, casting a spell on a target that already has the same spell only works if the new casting is stronger, and some spells can't be on the same target at once. For example, Bladesharp and Fireblade can't share a weapon, and neither can Protection and Shimmer on a person. When a spell has no effect for one of these reasons, Apply tells you why and the card records it. Only the target's owner sees the reason.

The world setting Recast Spells Take Effect chooses the rule: Only if stronger (the rules as written) or Always, where every new casting replaces the old one.

Spell effects on the token​

A token's spell effects are shown at the top of the token HUD's status palette, above the condition markers, with their name, the item they're on and the time left. The GM can click one to open it, right-click to remove it, or change its level on the effect's sheet.

The GM can also add a spell effect without casting, for example for a spell cast before the scene started. Click the + in the same section, search for the spell, set its level, and click Add.

Token status markers​

Because spells now show on tokens as spell effects, the token HUD's status palette no longer holds spell markers. Protection, Strength, Bladesharp and the other spell statuses are gone, and so are Ironhand, Parry and the 1–6 counters. The counters were a way to note a spell's level, which the spell effect's name now shows.

The palette now has general condition markers only. The new ones are Helpless, Concentrating, In Spirit Combat, Discorporate, Possessed, Invisible, Flying and Burning, and Restrained is now called Immobilized.

Upgrading

A spell status that is already on a token when you upgrade stays on it as an ordinary effect until it runs out. You can also delete it from the actor's Effects tab. For a spell that has no spell effect yet, add a plain effect on the actor's Effects tab.

Active Effects That Change an Actor's Items​

Active Effects that change a value on the actor's items have a new, more versatile key format. Before, such a change had to use the Custom change type and could only add to the value. Now it can use all of Foundry's change types. For example, it can raise one skill, add armor to every hit location, or improve the weapon it's on. Start the change's key with @, followed by which items to change, a :, and the path of the value:

KeyChangesExample
@<rqid>:system.<path>The actor's item with that rqid@i.skill.dodge:system.baseChance, add 10
@~<regex>:system.<path>Every item whose rqid matches the regular expression@~^i\.hit-location\.:system.naturalAp, add 2
@.:system.<path>The item the effect is on@.:system.effect.add.melee.attack on a weapon, add 10
  • Change types: these changes use Foundry's normal types, Add, Multiply, Override, Upgrade and Downgrade, just like any other key. Keys that don't start with @ work exactly as they do in Foundry.
  • Weapon bonuses: the system.effect.add values are bonuses added on top of a value. A weapon has them for melee and missile attack, parry and damage. The damage bonus is new in this release, and a weapon's rolled damage never goes below 0.
  • Spell level: in an effect that comes from a spell, @level in the value is the level the spell was cast at. For example, Bladesharp adds @level * 5 to attack.
  • Warnings: a key that can't be used, such as a mistyped rqid, an rqid the actor has no item for, or a change type a bonus can't use, shows the GM a warning saying what's wrong. When the world loads, these are collected into one summary.

See rqid: Active Effects for the bonus values you can change and more examples.

Changed keys

Before this release, a key like i.skill.dodge:system.baseChance on the Custom change type changed an item. The world migration rewrites these to the new form, @i.skill.dodge:system.baseChance on Add, so they keep doing the same thing. A key in the old form that the migration can't reach, such as one typed afterwards or in a module's compendium, shows the GM a warning and does nothing until it's updated.

Resisted Spells and Resistance Rolls​

A spell that the target can resist, like Sleep, Befuddle or Slow, now checks its target before any magic points are spent. It asks for confirmation if you have no target (a cast on yourself), and stops if you have more than one. The cast then posts a single card to the target's owner, who chooses Resist or Accept. The resistance roll pits the caster's POW against the target's POW on the resistance table. The target doesn't see which spell it is until it takes effect.

The GM can also send any resistance roll to a player, for example STR against a trap, from the actor sheet's Combat tab, the combat tracker, the Actors sidebar, or a Token HUD button. The Token HUD button can be turned off in the settings.

Start a New Actor from a Template​

Foundry's Create Actor dialog has a new Start from choice. Blank works as before, and Choose template... opens a list of template actors from the world and the compendiums. The wiki module provides templates for adventurers, like "Human (Base)" and "Mostali (Dwarf)", and for creatures, like animals, chaos monsters, elementals, mounts and spirits. Search the list by name, or filter it by class, role and species. The new actor is a copy of the template, with all its items.

See Creating an Adventurer for how to make your own actors show up as templates.

Hotbar Drag-and-Drop​

  • A weapon on the hotbar now opens its attack dialog, and a spirit or rune magic spell opens its cast dialog.
  • Items on the actor sheet's Combat tab, including Dodge, can be dragged to the hotbar.
  • A compendium on the hotbar opens it, a journal page opens its journal at that page, and a scene views it.
  • Ammunition that can't attack on its own, like arrows, and folders show a warning instead of making a macro that doesn't work.

For Content Creators​

  • Spells link to their effect with an Effect field on the spell sheet, next to the description link. Spells already on actors get their link from the compendium during the world migration.
  • Spells have a Resisted By field that says how a target can resist them. So far, only a resistance roll of POW against POW is supported; the other options are shown but not used yet.
  • Spells record what they can be cast on and what kind of effect they have, in two new fields.
  • Errors and warnings now carry a code like RQG-W-0012, which makes them easier to report and look up.

Other Bug Fixes​

  • Let any owner of an actor drag-reorder its Spirit and Rune Magic spells, not just the GM
  • Sort Spell Matrix spells in with the actor's own Spirit Magic spells, marked with the matrix item's image and a tooltip, instead of listing them separately
  • Stop the Rqid batch editor's progress from filling the screen with notifications
  • Bring back the GM-only "is creature" checkbox on the new actor sheet
  • Update the attack dialog when you change target while it's open
  • Give embedded Spirit Magic spells their rqid during the world migration, so they can be matched to the compendium
